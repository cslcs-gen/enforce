Report Overview

Executive Summary

9 prioritised AI problem statements for the Health Ministry enforcement ecosystem.

This report presents 9 prioritised AI problem statements developed for the Health Ministry’s enforcement division, spanning six key domains: field inspection operations, complaint management, online enforcement intelligence, professional conduct oversight, regulatory knowledge management, and licence lifecycle monitoring. Each problem statement is grounded in global research drawn from the FDA (USA), US DOJ, UK Care Quality Commission, Singapore MOH/HSA, Australia TGA/AHPRA, WHO/EMA, and peer-reviewed academic literature published between 2024 and 2026. Problem statements are individually scored against four evaluation dimensions — impact, feasibility, urgency, and proof-of-concept speed — to support prioritised investment and sequencing decisions.
